Account deletion

Delete your Type Detective account

Developer: Md Shahed Uddin Emon

In the app (fastest)

Open Type Detective and go to Settings → Account → Delete account, then confirm. Your account and its cloud copy are deleted straight away, and the device starts fresh.

By email

If you no longer have the game installed, email shahedemon.pro@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Type Detective account". Include the email of the Google or Apple account you linked. We'll confirm and delete within 30 days.

If you never linked Google or Apple, your account is anonymous and we can't find it from an email address. Reinstall the game on the same device and use the in-app option, or send us the approximate date you last played and your device model and we'll do our best to locate it.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (anonymous ID and any linked Google or Apple email).
  • Your cloud save: progress, collection, streaks, in-game currency balances and settings.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Purchase records kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat, for as long as tax and accounting law requires. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings.
  • Analytics and crash data, which isn't linked to your email, expires on its own (up to 14 months for analytics, about 90 days for crash reports).

Progress saved only on your device is removed when you uninstall the game.
