Privacy Policy

Rainbloom Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026

  • You can play without giving us your name or email. The game starts with an anonymous account.
  • If you choose to link Google or Apple, we receive the email address that provider shares, so your progress can follow you to other devices.
  • Approximate location is used for the weather or sky only and is never stored, synced, or used for ads or analytics.
  • We use Google Firebase (accounts, cloud save, analytics, crash reports), RevenueCat (purchases) and Google AdMob (ads in the free version). We don't sell your data.
  • You can delete your account and cloud data in the app at any time: Settings (the bench) → Account → Delete my cloud data.

3. Device permissions

Location

Rainbloom asks for approximate location only (iOS reduced accuracy; Android coarse location). On your device it is snapped to a grid cell about 25–30 km wide, and only that cell's centre is sent to the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway, api.met.no) to get the forecast. Your location is not stored, logged, synced, or shared with analytics or advertising. You can deny the permission and pick a climate by hand instead.

Notifications

Reminders are optional, scheduled on your device, and you can turn them off in the game or in your device settings. We don't use push-notification servers.

What the game never accesses

Rainbloom does not access your precise location, camera, microphone, contacts or photo library.

4. Advertising and your consent

The free version of Rainbloom can show ads from Google AdMob. Players can remove them with a purchase.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before any ad loads, the game shows Google's consent message (Google User Messaging Platform). Ads are personalised only if you agree, and you can change your choice later from the game's settings.
  • iPhone and iPad: the game doesn't ask to track you across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency), so it never accesses your device's advertising identifier.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.
  • US state privacy laws: we don't sell personal information for money. Where personalised advertising counts as "sharing", you can opt out with the consent controls above or by limiting ad tracking on your device.

Google's use of data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

5. Legal bases (EEA and UK players)

  • Contract: your account, cloud save, sync and purchases, which you need to play as you chose.
  • Legitimate interests: crash reports, basic analytics and non-personalised ads, to keep the game working and free to play.
  • Consent: personalised advertising, and linking a Google or Apple account. You can withdraw consent at any time.

6. Who we share data with

Only the service providers named above, who process data for us under their own terms and data protection agreements: Google (Firebase, AdMob), RevenueCat, Apple and Google Play (payments), MET Norway (forecast requests with approximate coordinates only), and authorities when the law requires it. These providers may store data in the United States and other countries; transfers out of the EEA and UK rely on the providers' Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent safeguards.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account and cloud progress: until you delete your account. If you want inactive data removed, email us.
  • Analytics: no longer than 14 months (the Google Analytics retention setting).
  • Crash reports: about 90 days.
  • Purchase records: as long as needed to give you what you paid for and to meet tax and accounting law; the stores keep their own records.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Delete: in the app, Settings (the bench) → Account → Delete my cloud data deletes your account and cloud copy straight away. You can also ask by email.
  • Access, correct, export or object: email us and we'll answer within 30 days.
  • Ads: change your ad consent in the game's settings or your device settings, or buy the ad-free option.
  • Complain: you may contact your local data protection authority.

Deleting the game from your device does not delete your cloud account; use the in-app option or email us. Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google; deleting your account doesn't cancel them (see the Terms).

9. Children

Rainbloom is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We don't knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, email us and we'll delete it.

10. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and each player's cloud data can be read or written only by that player's own account. No method is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the game.
